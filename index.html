Questions

Is AI video allowed on a listing?

Many MLSs and listing sites accept video tours, but rules differ, so check your MLS's virtual-tour rules. Some want tours without agent branding, so every listing video also comes in an MLS-unbranded version. We add a short "created using AI" line to every video.

What about California's AB 723?

In California, AB 723 requires a disclosure next to digitally altered media and a way to see the original photos. As the listing agent you add that disclosure and link your original photos, and we'll remind you.

Can you add furniture or make the lawn greener?

No. Our videos show the property as it is, which keeps your listing accurate and compliant.

How long does it take?

Within 3 business days of receiving usable photos.

What do I receive?

MP4 files: horizontal (16:9) for listings, websites and YouTube, and vertical (9:16) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Listing videos also come in an MLS-unbranded version with no names, logos or contact details, for MLSs that don't allow branding.

Can I use it on Airbnb?

Airbnb doesn't allow video in listings. Hosts use our stay tours on Vrbo, their own booking website, social media and in messages to guests who've booked.

How do I pay?

Through the payment link for your package (the "Order now" button above). Payments are handled securely by Stripe.

Can I get changes?

Yes, one round of revisions is included. Reply to the delivery email with what you'd like changed.
