Questions

Is AI video allowed on a listing?

Many MLSs and listing sites accept video tours, but rules differ, so check your MLS's virtual-tour rules. Listing videos also come in an unbranded version for MLSs that don't allow branding, and we add a short "created using AI" line to every video.

What about California's AB 723?

In California, AB 723 requires a disclosure next to digitally altered media and a way to see the original photos. As the listing agent you add that disclosure and link your original photos, and we'll remind you.

Can you add furniture or make the lawn greener?

No. Our videos show the property as it is, which keeps your listing accurate and compliant.

Can I use it on Airbnb?

Airbnb doesn't allow video in listings. Hosts use our stay tours on Vrbo, their own booking website, social media and in messages to guests who've booked.

How long does it take?

Within 24 hours of receiving usable photos, any day of the week.

What do I receive?

MP4 files: horizontal (16:9) for listings, websites and YouTube, and vertical (9:16) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Listing and community videos also come in an MLS-unbranded version with no names, logos or contact details, for MLSs that don't allow branding.

Can I get changes?

Yes, one round of revisions is included. Reply to the delivery email with what you'd like changed.

How do I pay?

Through the payment link for your package. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
